## Key lessons logged

- `driver.use_system_fonts()` is mandatory for any text-bearing render — embedded DejaVu subsets panic otherwise (fixed at vieww-render/src/frame.rs:1096).
- The `alpha()` convention incident: caller passed 0–255-style values into a 0–1 API, making every translucent swatch opaque. The framework was innocent (verified against native/color.rs, target.rs, reference.rs); found via FILM_DUMP_CMDS command-stream dump. Same species as the 2550-vs-2551 incident in handoff.md.
- **The Rect-edges incident (round 3)**: `Rect::new` takes *edges* (left, top, right, bottom), not (x, y, w, h). Origin-anchored rects are identical either way, which masks the difference — offset rects silently render degenerate (zero/negative height hairlines, tick rails that never draw). Fixed with the `xywh()` helper; kinetic's tick rail and band rules re-rendered visibly for the first time.
- **The sampled-attack trap (round 3)**: a 0.1 s attack with 5.5/s decay is invisible at 16-frame sampling (0.75 s spacing) — the flash lives and dies between frames. Effects must be tuned against the harness's sampling cadence, not just against wall-clock time. damage's envelope now spans ~1.2 s.
- Aesthetic floor verified: 8/10 VLM distance to the author's own sheet-vf reference frame; round-3 props audit at 9/10 (spring, scrub, rackfocus, morph, endcard) and 7/10 (damage, after the envelope fix — full-res verification shows wash + border + corner ticks correct).
- Framework boundary confirmed: `Transform` is deliberately 2D affine only — true 3D requires manual projection in custom Painting closures (see three_d.rs).

## Round-4 key lessons

- **The display-pipeline ghost**: `#[must_use]` reads as `#ust_use]` through this session's tool output — the `[m` is eaten as an ANSI reset somewhere in the chain. The file was innocent; `rg "must_use"` vs `rg "#ust_use"` settled it. Always verify suspected corruption with a second instrument.
- **The baseline-bake incident**: geometry built once bakes its placement constants in. The wordmark Mark carries `BASELINE = 340` inside its paths; translating by a target y without subtracting the baked one put the letters off-canvas — and only the *reflection* was visible (an upside-down "vieww" reads "AIGMM": a mirrored w is an M). Fix: translate by `target_y − BASELINE`; mirror the placed path, not the raw one.
- **The collapsed-Positioned incident**: `Opacity::new(a).child(Positioned…)` renders at origin — a Positioned needs a positioning parent (a Stack), not a plain wrapper. Symptom: hero's caption text at top-left instead of bottom-right.
- **The U-14 last inch**: `ImageFilter` carried `blur_angle`, the rasterizer honoured it, `Filtered` never exposed it — the shatter plate completed the chain with `Filtered::with_blur_angle` (now in `vieww_base`).
- **The hero budget, this session's own number**: 1920×1080 · 4,703 shapes · 129 layers · 149 ms mean, 171 ms worst. A 10,800-frame master of frames like it is ~27 minutes of rendering. The constraint remains taste.

## Round-6 key lessons

- **The shape-count ceiling is not where anyone thought it was.** `galaxy` pushed **60,527 shapes through one frame in 101 ms** — thirteen times the hero frame's count at two-thirds of its cost. The hero's expense was never its shape count; it was the blurred layers and the big fills. Small rects and tiny circles are the cheap path: the `mandel` plate's 57,602 rects run at **69 ms**. "Nothing in the film is limited by the renderer" now has the count axis measured too — the constraint is taste, then memory, then time, in that order.
- **4K is a memory axis, not a speed axis.** `hero4k` renders the hero tree at 3840×2160 in 440–489 ms/frame — 3× the 1080p cost for 4× the pixels, sub-linear as expected. But a 4-frame run was SIGKILLed (exit −9, the OOM-killer's signature) on the 4 GB bench while the 2-frame run passes: the offscreen layer buffers at 4K accumulate across frames, and the mitigation is chunked passes. The tolerance finding for the master plan: render 4K in frame-chunks, or give the box RAM.
- **The audit-prompt is an instrument with its own failure mode.** The same `fadeaway` sheet scored 2/10 under a context-free prompt ("almost entirely black") and 9/10 under a prompt that named the plate's intent ("this is intentionally a dark sequence"). Same pixels, same model. The audit prompt is part of the instrument — for dark-theme plates it must carry the design intent, or it will flag every deliberate dark frame as a defect. Same species as the sampled-attack trap: the instrument's sampling, not the render, produces the finding.
- **A vertical vector has no yaw** (the `dir_turn` incident): rotating a direction "by yaw around Y" collapses when the direction IS Y — the perpendicular is zero and every child inherits the parent's line: a tree renders as a pole. Fixed with the orthonormal-frame construction (tilt away from `d` by pitch, in the compass direction yaw selects), plus apical dominance and a phototropism term (every child pulled 0.2 back toward +Y) — which is what separates a tree from a fern. Score went 6 → 4 → 8/10 across the iterations; the VLM's "shepherd's crook" note was the geometry telling the truth.
- **The window-slit incident (city)**: a face-aligned quad built by lerping "from the column at u" to "the column at u+s" renders as a one-sided sliver — windows present in the count, invisible in the frame. The shape count cannot tell you this; only the audit can. (Same genus as the Rect-edges and baseline-bake incidents: the arithmetic is legal, the geometry is wrong.)
- **OnceLock re-entry is a silent deadlock** (forest round 1): `total_segments()` computed by calling `grow()`, which called `total_segments()` — the first render hung without an error, a timeout with no diagnostics. The fix was to stop needing the count at all (a depth-wave reveal needs no census). When a render hangs with zero output, suspect re-entrant initialisation before suspecting the renderer.
- **Dark plates need bright anchors**: the audits that read best (avatar, blackhole, city, typo, fadeaway post-fix) all keep one bright element on stage — the lit contour, the photon ring, the windows' amber, the drop. The sea plate's 8/10 ceiling is the same lesson from below: the glints carry the frame when the water is dark.
- **The GIF-vs-mp4 receipt (round-6 addendum)**: every plate now also ships as a palette-optimised `anim.gif` (640 px, two-pass palette, `floyd_steinberg` error diffusion) so the motion loops inline on GitHub with zero codecs. The dither was settled by the audit loop, not chosen: the first bayer:5 recipe — picked from an aurora-only size sample — was flagged DEGRADED by the A/B VLM on aurora's gradients, and the full bake-off then showed floyd is *smaller* than bayer on 34 of 36 plates anyway (the single-plate calibration, in miniature, is the sampled-attack lesson again). Measured honestly: all 36 GIFs total 16.1 MB against 1.4 MB for the same plates as crf-23 mp4 — **H.264 is ~11.3× smaller, and that number goes in the honesty ledger, not the marketing.** The GIF ships anyway because it is the right *repo* artifact (one click, no codec, no player); its byte price is printed per plate in every `metrics.txt` (`gif=` line). One known limit: `aurora` (intentional grain over smooth ramps) exceeds the GIF 256-colour floor at every dither/width tried — its audit surface of record stays the full-res sheet.

## Round-7 key lessons

- **The 8-stop gradient ceiling (U-23, the round's catch)**: `Gradient::with_stops` silently `.take(8)`s longer input — a 256-stop spectrum rendered as a flat orange panel because the sorted first eight stops of a rainbow are all red-orange. Pixel-verified uniform before the workaround. No assert fires on *count*, only on *order*. Same species as U-17: a failure that looks like a decision. Filed in todo-upgrades with the fix candidates.
- **A spiral cannot carry a picture (the megapath incident)**: pitch sampled at the walking point modulates a spiral's *turn-averaged* spacing — the landscape washed out to a 9–15% near-uniform ink field, measured by row-scan. A serpentine scan whose next row integrates pointwise gives *exact* local spacing; the rows flow around the moon like field lines. 4/10 → 9/10. The geometry instrument (row ink scan) caught what the eye suspected.
- **Memory is flat at 720p across 256 frames**: the endurance plate's RSS series reads 38.3→39.0 MiB with raster stable at 25.5 ms ±2% — the hero4k accumulator is a 4K-scale fact, not a general one. The next memory argument starts from this curve.
- **ffmpeg's split graph queues, and the cgroup notices**: a one-pass `palettegen|paletteuse` GIF over 256 full-res PNGs was SIGKILLed on the 4 GB bench — the `[b]` branch parks every frame until the palette exists. The house recipe is now two-pass (palette to a file, then frames through it), and strided assemblies hardlink their subset first. GIF determinism held: the harness's output byte-matched the manual run.
- **The census earns its keep on round 7 too**: swarm's 2,400 bird triangles reported 38,419 open-subpath fills — every one missing `close()`, pixel-correct chord closure, invisible in the audit. The counter caught what the VLM could not (U-22's species, second working day).
- **Full-res is the audit surface for fine detail**: han's brush-stroke taper was invisible at 480-px sheet scale (6/10) and clear at 1280×720 (8/10) — the audit-prompt lesson's spatial twin. Fine-detail plates audit on a full frame, not only the sheet.
- **CJK needs no framework door**: `FontFamily::Named("LXGW WenKai")` + `use_system_fonts()` shaped real hanzi on the first try, Latin and Han mixed in one frame, ~107 glyph runs/frame. The tofu failure mode has a measurable signature (near-uniform per-cell ink), which is now a probe.

## Round-9 key lessons

**The round's rule: every plate is a law of nature run as a machine, and the receipt must close the law's own books** — fringes vs λL/d, Strouhal vs 0.2, σ vs √(npq), M(T) vs Onsager, ΣFy vs zero, MET vs ΣpT. Twelve laws, twelve closures. The round's other finding is darker and better: **the VLM audits scored four broken plates 8.5–9/10** — galton's balls never landed (the bell curve the audit admired was all in-flight dust), neural's net was dead (loss = ln 2 behind a pretty field), truss's member forces were 90× the applied load (behind a beautiful bridge), ising's magnet never ordered (M = 0.16 behind nice domains). The audit layer measures looks; the receipt layer measures truth. This round the receipts did the catching.

- **The fringe factor-of-two (quantum)**: the intensity fringe term is cos(φ) with φ = 2π·d·y/(λL) — the full path-difference phase, one full turn per fringe. The first cut used cos(φ/2), the *amplitude* term, putting fringes at 2·λL/d — and the probe measured 93 px against a formula claiming 46. A receipt disagreeing with itself is the tell; the physics was wrong, not the instrument.
- **The Rect-edges incident, second generation (quantum)**: the run-divider rail was built as `Rect::new(0, 362, w, 26)` — edges, not x+y+w+h — a full-width near-black rect over y 26..362 that quietly painted the entire top run "wiped by the rasterizer." Three hours of instrument suspicion (including a minimal repro that ACQUITTED the renderer's negative-bounds layer path — bounds are clamped correctly at composite) ended at FILM_DUMP_CMDS cmd[033], the rail's true extent. Round 3 logged this species with the words "origin-anchored rects are identical either way, masking the difference" — the prediction held.
- **An instrument's aperture is its resolution (quantum)**: the screen-strip probe first read 61 "bright bands" at 4.2 px spacing (the histogram bars' own pixel noise), then plateau edges every 30 px. A 9-px two-pass box with a prominence requirement read 6 bands at 50.4 px — +8.6% against λL/d. Round 8's mirror-pair lesson, restated: the probe must be engineered like the plate.
- **The checkerboard wake (smoke)**: a collocated velocity grid with central-difference projection has an odd-even mode the solver cannot see — the field alternates ±2 cells/step and the dye goes nowhere. The staggered MAC grid is not a style choice; it is what makes the mode impossible. And the staggered world has its own arithmetic: with DIV = −(∇·U) the Gauss–Seidel update is P = (Σ + DIV)/4, and the face gradients are plain differences — the 0.5 that belongs to collocated central differences will explode the field to 10⁴⁷. Both sign-family errors were caught by the same five-minute Python replica before any Rust was burned.
- **The global phase breaks the group (crystal)**: a common phase Φ on all five waves maps under 36° rotation to ψ₋Φ — the C10 symmetry the plate promises survives only at Φ = 0. The plate's own probe caught the plate's own broken promise (corr(36°) < corr(60°)); the fix was to animate what preserves the group (the precession — a rigid rotation — and the band thresholds — a uniform function of the field) and animate nothing else.
- **The disconnected readout (neural)**: a stray index helper made the output layer read w3[0]·h2[0] — one hidden unit in sixteen — and the net trained faithfully to loss = ln 2 with 0.6% accuracy. Under it lay a second real problem: full-batch descent sits in the two-spirals saddle forever. The fix needed both the full readout AND mini-batch noise (the shuffles are seeded — the replay stays a pure function of its constants). 1.5 turns, lr 0.05, momentum 0.9: **the plateau holds to ≈ epoch 1,500, then the loss falls 0.65 → 0.17 → 0.004 inside 300 epochs.** The click is the film beat.
- **The never-landing balls (galton)**: FALL_SPAN read as per-row time made the ladder cost 3.84 timelines — no ball ever landed, and the receipt said "landed 0 · airborne 6000" to our face. The sheet still audited 8.5/10. Units are geometry.
- **The frozen magnet (ising)**: cooling to T = 0.14 with 90 sweeps/stage freezes at M = 0.16 — too cold for domain walls to move, too few sweeps to coarsen; the magnet never magnetised and the receipt printed it. T_COLD = 0.5 with 240 sweeps: M(T) walks 0.05 → 0.13 → 0.87 → 1.00, the transition landing between T = 2.44 and 2.18 — **beside Onsager's 2.269, from a 120×68 quenched finite lattice**.
- **The phantom-force solve (truss)**: the method-of-joints accumulator summed every solved member at every joint — connected or not — along a direction invented for the occasion; forces compounded to 90× the applied load and the receipt printed them. Beneath it, the ΣFy "check" was a tautology (one reaction derived from the other — a check that cannot fail checks nothing). Fixed: connected-only accumulation, both reactions from independent moment equations, y-down convention throughout (the first cut mixed up-positive loads with y-down geometry and solved the bridge with gravity inverted — the textbook answer is the test). Now: **bottom chord tension +40.1, top compression −40.2, every Pratt diagonal in tension, ΣFy residual 10⁻⁵.**
- **The Strouhal, printed beside the literature**: the street organises at St ≈ 0.16 against the famous ≈0.2 — measured from the solver's own pressure field, printed with the comparison, left standing as what this resolution honestly does. The receipt's job is the number, not the compliment.
